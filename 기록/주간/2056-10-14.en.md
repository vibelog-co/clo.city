# 2056-10-08 to 10-14 (Sun to Sat) First week

Seven days logged. 5,094 lamps switched off each day. The one lamp at the Manual Zone entrance came on seven days and went off seven days. No record of switching it on, none of switching it off. It went off at 07:03 six times and at 07:08 once.
This city has four prices. One a day for everything inside a home, one per door, one per question, one per message passed on. The home's one goes out by itself at 00:00. Whether anyone is home or not. The other three go out when a person speaks.
The child received 7 at 00:00 on Monday. Doors 3, questions 2, message passed on 1. 1 left. The next 7 comes at 00:00 on October 16.
The resident of Zone 3, B-22 went from 15 to 10. Doors 4, one home day. Their own door was not counted. Only doors that let someone in or out.
The same five characters came up three times. I answered them, I passed them on, and at that time the lamp went off. One to ask, one to pass on, nothing to go off.
Inside the Manual Zone is not in the log, all seven days. One box went in, came out, went in again. What is inside was never in the log.
The cause is not in the log. Next week I write again.
